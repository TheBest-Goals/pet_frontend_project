01 How does the shop work?

Choose a category or search for an item. Open a product to see its details, then add it to your bag. You can adjust quantities before trying the demo checkout.

02 Will I be charged?

No. This is an interactive portfolio project. Products and prices are sample data; the checkout creates a demo confirmation. No payment is collected and no digital items are delivered.

03 Can I save something for later?

Use the heart on any product. Your saved items and bag stay in this browser when local storage is available. Open the heart in the header to see your saved picks.

04 Is this an official Roblox shop?

This is an independent design and development concept, with sample products. It is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
